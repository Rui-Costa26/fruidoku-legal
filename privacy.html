Fruidoku Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

In short: Fruidoku has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your progress stays on your device (and in your own iCloud, if sync is on). The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which processes some device data to deliver and measure ads. Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent first.

Who we are

Fruidoku is a logic puzzle game for iPhone, developed by Rui Costa ("the developer"). Contact: ruipgc26@gmail.com

What Fruidoku stores

Your solved levels, stars, times, saved games, hint balance and settings are stored inside the app on your device. The developer has no server and cannot see this information.

iCloud sync

When iCloud sync is available and iCloud is turned on for Fruidoku, your progress is also kept in your private iCloud database so it can follow you to your other devices. That database belongs to your Apple Account; Apple manages it and the developer cannot access it. You can turn iCloud off for Fruidoku in iOS Settings.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Fruidoku shows a banner during play, a full-screen ad when you run out of lives, and, only when you choose, a short video that earns a hint. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may process data such as your IP address (and the approximate location it implies), device and app information, an app-specific device identifier, and how you interact with ads.

  • Consent: in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Fruidoku shows Google's consent form before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time in Fruidoku → Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Tracking: Fruidoku does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites, so iOS does not give it the advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Purchases

If hint packs are offered, they are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive your name, payment details or Apple Account information. Fruidoku records only how many hints you have.

What Fruidoku does not do

  • It has no user accounts and no sign-in.
  • It does not include analytics tools of its own.
  • It does not sell your data.

Children

Fruidoku is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Your rights

Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing for the developer to export or delete: deleting the app (and its iCloud data in iOS Settings) removes your progress. For data processed by Google for advertising, use Privacy choices in the app or Google's controls linked above. You can contact the developer with any privacy question, and you may complain to your data protection authority (in Portugal, the CNPD).

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with an updated date.
